# Quick Start Guide

## Welcome to SoundGarden! 🎵

Create immersive spatial audio soundscapes with just a few taps.

## Getting Started (30 seconds)

### 1. Launch & Welcome
- Open SoundGarden
- Swipe through the welcome screens
- Choose a demo scene OR start from scratch

### 2. Add Your First Sound
- Tap the **"Add Sound"** button
- Select from built-in ambient sounds:
  - 🌳 Forest ambience
  - 🌊 Ocean waves
  - 🌧️ Rain
  - 🔥 Fireplace
  - And more!

### 3. Position the Sound
- **Drag** the sound source around the circular canvas
- Place it anywhere within the circle
- The position creates spatial audio effects

### 4. Adjust Settings
- **Tap** a sound source to select it
- Control panel slides up from bottom
- Adjust:
  - 🔊 **Volume** - How loud
  - 🎭 **Reverb** - Space/echo effect
  - ⚖️ **Pan** - Left/right balance

### 5. Play & Enjoy
- Tap the **play button** on any source
- Or use the **master play** at the top
- Put on **headphones** for best experience!

## Tips & Tricks

### 🎧 For Best Results
- Use headphones or AirPods
- Spatial audio works best with stereo headphones
- Close your eyes to really feel the space

### 🎨 Creating Scenes
- **Multiple sources**: Add up to 10 sounds
- **Layering**: Combine different sounds for ambience
- **Distance**: Further from center = quieter
- **Spread out**: Surround yourself with sound

### 💾 Saving Your Work
1. Tap the **gear icon** (⚙️) in top-right
2. Select **"Save Scene"**
3. Access saved scenes anytime

### 🎭 Quick Presets
In the control panel:
- Tap **"Dry"** for no reverb
- Tap **"Light Reverb"** for small room
- Tap **"Heavy Reverb"** for large hall
- Tap **"Cathedral"** for maximum space

## Common Questions

**Q: Why can't I hear spatial audio?**
A: Make sure you're using headphones! Spatial audio works best with stereo headphones or AirPods.

**Q: How many sounds can I add?**
A: Up to 10 simultaneous sound sources.

**Q: Can I use my own audio files?**
A: Yes! Tap "Add Sound" → "Choose Audio File" and select MP3, WAV, M4A, or AAC files.

**Q: Why do sounds get quieter when I move them?**
A: That's spatial audio working! Sounds further from center are naturally attenuated.

**Q: Can I use this for sleep?**
A: Absolutely! Create a relaxing scene, start playback, and the audio continues in the background.

**Q: What's the circle boundary?**
A: It represents your spatial audio field. Sounds must stay within this area.

## Pro Tips

### 🎯 Advanced Positioning
- **Corners** = maximum stereo separation
- **Center** = mono, direct sound
- **Edges** = strong spatial effect
- **Near center** = more intimate

### 🔊 Volume Strategy
- Start all sources at 50%
- Adjust relative to each other
- Main sound = louder
- Background = 20-40%
- Accents = 10-30%

### 🎭 Reverb Artistry
- **Dry** (0%) = close, intimate
- **Light** (20%) = small room
- **Medium** (50%) = concert hall
- **Heavy** (80%) = cathedral
- Mix different levels for depth

### 🎨 Layering Sounds
1. Start with **base layer** (e.g., ocean)
2. Add **mid layer** (e.g., wind)
3. Add **detail layer** (e.g., birds)
4. Balance volumes carefully

### ⚡ Performance
- More sources = more CPU
- Pause unused sources
- Close other apps for best performance

## Troubleshooting

### Audio won't play
1. Check device volume
2. Check silent mode switch
3. Restart the app
4. Check audio permissions in Settings

### Stuttering/glitches
1. Close other apps
2. Reduce number of active sources
3. Restart device
4. Update to latest iOS

### Import fails
1. Check file format (MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC only)
2. Check file isn't corrupted
3. Try a different file

### App crashes
1. Force quit and reopen
2. Restart device
3. Check available storage
4. Update app
